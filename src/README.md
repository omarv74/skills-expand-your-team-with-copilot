# Mergington High School Activities

A website application that allows students to view and sign up for extracurricular activities at Mergington High School.

## Features

- **View activities** – Browse all available extracurricular activities displayed as cards
- **Search** – Search activities by name or description
- **Filter by category** – Filter activities by Sports, Arts, Academic, Community, or Technology
- **Filter by day** – Show only activities that meet on a specific day of the week
- **Filter by time** – Show activities by time slot (Before School, After School, or Weekend)
- **Filter by difficulty** – Filter activities by Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced level
- **Student sign-up** – Teachers can register students for activities
- **Teacher login** – Secure login for teachers and administrators to manage registrations
- **Dark mode** – Toggle between light and dark display themes
- **Social sharing** – Share any activity via a copied link, email, or X (Twitter)
